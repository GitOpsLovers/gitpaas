## 🌟 What is GitPaaS?

GitPaaS is your own hosting platform. You install it on a server that belongs to you, you connect the place where your code lives, and you publish your applications from a web page.

No cloud bill that grows every month. No console with a hundred menus. Your server, your code, your rules.

## 💡 What do you use it for?

- 🚀 **Publish an application.** Choose a repository, choose a branch, press the button.
- 🔁 **Publish it again.** A new version goes online in the same way.
- 👀 **See what happens.** The log appears while your application starts, and it stays for later.
- 🗂️ **Keep order.** Group your applications by client, by team or by idea.
- 🔐 **Sleep well.** Your keys and your passwords stay on your machine.

## 🙋 Who is it for?

- The developer who has a small server and many side projects.
- The team that wants its own platform instead of a rented one.
- Anybody who likes to own the place where the code runs.

## 🚀 Install it

You need a Linux server. Open a terminal on it, and paste this line:

```bash
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/GitOpsLovers/gitpaas/main/scripts/install.sh | sh
```

The installer asks you a few questions:

1. **Your email.** It becomes your administrator account.
2. **Your domain (optional).** Give one, and your platform gets a secure address. Give none, and you open it by the address of the server.

At the end, the installer shows a password. **Copy it.** Then open the address on the screen, and sign in.

That is all. Your platform is ready.

## 🧭 Your first application

1. **Connect your code.** GitPaaS talks to GitHub today.
2. **Create a space for the application**, and tell it which repository and which branch to use.
3. **Press Deploy**, and watch it go live.
